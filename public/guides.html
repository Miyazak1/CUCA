After offer

Visa, JW form, and arrival steps are part of the application

This is a typical sequence. Exact requirements vary by country, university, current policy, and embassy or consulate instruction.

01Admission notice

Confirm the offer, registration date, and any conditional requirements.

02JW201 / JW202 form

Use the confirmation form for study in China when required for the visa route.

03X1 / X2 visa

Study period and current rules determine which student visa path applies.

04Physical exam

Some students need a Foreigner Physical Examination Form or local health verification.

05Registration and permit

Bring required documents and follow university registration and residence procedures.
