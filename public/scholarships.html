Common scholarship misunderstandings

Keep expectations realistic before investing weeks in an application route.

Does scholarship mean admission?

No. Funding route, program admission, and university review are related but separate decisions.

Does full funding cover everything?

Not always. Tuition, accommodation, stipend, insurance, and renewal rules must be checked in the notice.

Can I apply without a final program?

Usually you should connect funding to a real university and program route before preparing documents.
