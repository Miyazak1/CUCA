# School-evidence city draft wave 01

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: unreviewed drafts; publication is not authorized

Run `npm run catalog:cities:school-evidence-drafts` after regenerating the city inventory and acquiring registered sources.

## Scope

This wave organizes already-acquired official university sources for thirteen cities:

- Shanghai — official evidence from the ten linked public-university records
- Guiyang — Guizhou Medical University-led evidence
- Changsha — Central South University-led evidence
- Xi'an — three linked schools; acquired evidence is currently scholarship/admissions-led
- Harbin — Harbin Institute of Technology-led evidence
- Changchun — Jilin University-led evidence
- Jinan — Shandong University-led evidence
- Xiangtan — Xiangtan University-led evidence
- Shenzhen — Southern University of Science and Technology-led evidence
- Qingdao — Ocean University of China-led evidence
- Kunming — Yunnan University of Finance and Economics-led evidence, including an official accommodation range of CNY 4,800–6,000 per person per year (CNY 400–500 monthly equivalent)
- Qinhuangdao — Yanshan University 2026 international admission guide evidence
- Nanning — Guangxi Medical University 2026 undergraduate admission evidence, including official accommodation of CNY 6,000 per person per year (CNY 500 monthly equivalent)

Each output contains source metadata and checksums, a dated CUAC catalog-relation snapshot, English source copy, and Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian, Malay and Arabic drafts. The outputs intentionally contain no city-wide cost estimate and no inferred municipal facts. Kunming and Nanning contain accommodation-only monthly equivalents derived transparently from official annual dormitory prices; these must not be presented as total student living budgets.

## Interpretation boundary

These are structured school-evidence drafts, not complete city profiles. The internal school/program counts help prioritize production but are not official municipal statistics. A city can advance to a complete evidence pack only after current municipal statistics, international-arrival/service guidance and defensible student-cost evidence are acquired.

The registered Xi'an and Changchun statistical sources failed the deterministic collector at TLS negotiation on 2026-09-22. Shanghai municipal sources returned HTTP 403 and the Guiyang English municipal portal presented an invalid-hostname certificate. Certificate validation and host protections were not bypassed; their school-evidence drafts use only successfully acquired official university material.
