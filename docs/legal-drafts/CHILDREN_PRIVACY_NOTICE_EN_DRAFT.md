## child_and_guardian — Children, guardians and scope

These Rules apply when a student under 14 requests a CUAC student account. CUAC asks the child's parent or other legal guardian to give consent through a separately delivered one-time link.

Before consent, CUAC retains only a locked registration record needed to complete that decision. It cannot sign in, has no student role, and cannot create favourites, application plans or other student business records.

Students aged 14 or older are covered by CUAC's general privacy notice. This distinction does not mean that students aged 14 to 17 cease to receive the protections applicable to minors.

## data_categories — Information we process

Before guardian consent, we process:

- the child's registration email address, display name and one-way password hash;
- the declared age band, without collecting a full date of birth or identity document;
- the guardian's email address and relationship category;
- the one-time request identifier, token hash, request time and expiry;
- one-way summaries of the source IP and browser information used for abuse prevention; and
- the guardian's decision, decision time, and the exact language, version, digest and publication revision of these Rules.

After activation, depending on features the child chooses to use, we may also process:

- account email, display name, language and security status;
- study preferences, target level and intended locations;
- saved universities, programs and scholarships;
- applicant basics, education history, and planned or reported tests entered by the child;
- specific universities, programs, intakes, teaching languages and notes in an application plan; and
- sign-in, security, audit and in-app notification records.

The current release does not receive, store or submit passports, transcripts, recommendation letters or other application materials on behalf of a student, and live payment is not enabled. If that product boundary changes, we will update these Rules and obtain renewed guardian consent where required.

## purposes_and_bases — Purposes, necessity and basis

We process children's personal information only to:

- establish the express consent of a parent or other legal guardian;
- create and protect the student account and provide sign-in, recovery and security functions;
- display public admissions information and save preferences, favourites and application plans chosen by the child;
- hand off an identified program interest to the relevant university only when the child initiates and completes the required confirmation;
- respond to access, correction, deletion, account closure and consent-withdrawal requests;
- prevent fraud, automated registration, credential abuse and other security incidents; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

Personal information of a child under 14 is sensitive personal information. CUAC processes it only for a specific and necessary purpose with strict protective measures, and uses the guardian action described here to obtain the consent required for children's personal information. A material change beyond the disclosed purpose or scope must be reassessed and, where required, receive renewed consent.

## guardian_consent — How guardian consent is obtained and recorded

After the child enters a guardian email address, CUAC sends a one-time decision link to that address. The link expires after 72 hours by default. The recipient must confirm that they are the child's parent or other legal guardian, review these Rules, and actively choose to approve or decline.

CUAC records the exact notice language, version, content digest, publication revision, decision and decision time. The link credential is single-use and only its hash is stored by the server. Approval does not automatically sign in the guardian or child.

CUAC does not activate the account if the notice has been withdrawn or expired, its content no longer matches, or the link is invalid. If the guardian declines or the link expires, the locked registration credential is terminated and scrubbed.

Email confirmation reduces impersonation risk but cannot resolve every complex family situation. If there is a dispute, conflicting guardianship claim or reasonable sign of fraud, CUAC may pause the request and require risk-proportionate additional verification. CUAC does not request identity cards, household registration documents, facial images or birth certificates by default.

## recipients — Who may receive information

Only people and service providers that need the information for the purposes above may access it, including:

- authorised CUAC privacy, security and customer-support personnel operating under least privilege;
- processors providing cloud hosting, databases, object storage, email delivery, security protection and logging;
- the relevant university or its authorised staff when the child deliberately initiates an identified school handoff; and
- recipients required by law or by a lawful request from a competent authority.

CUAC requires processors to follow defined purposes, scope, duration, security responsibilities and assistance obligations. The current release does not send student application materials to universities because CUAC does not collect those materials.

CUAC will not make a cross-border transfer without the required assessment, notice, consent or other lawful procedure.

## retention — Retention and end-of-life handling

- An incomplete guardian-consent link expires after 72 hours by default. After decline or expiry, CUAC removes the password hash, raw guardian email and token hash, retaining only minimum outcome evidence for an approved security and compliance period.
- Active account data is retained only while needed to provide account functions. Account closure, withdrawal or a valid deletion request is handled under these Rules and applicable law.
- Security logs, audit evidence, backups and legally required records are retained only for the shortest period needed for security, dispute handling or legal obligations, with restricted access and scheduled deletion or anonymisation.
- Information held by processors is kept for the shortest contractually defined period, then deleted or returned.

This draft must not be published until that schedule is approved.

## security — How we protect children's information

CUAC uses measures proportionate to risk, including transport and storage protections, strong password hashing, one-time tokens, encrypted email queues, least privilege, role and tenant isolation, reauthentication for sensitive actions, access and action auditing, rate limiting, vulnerability remediation, backups and incident response.

Personnel access must be necessary for their work and remains subject to authorisation and audit. If children's personal information is or may be leaked, damaged or lost, CUAC will take immediate remedial measures and notify affected children, guardians and competent authorities as required.

No online service can promise absolute security. Guardians and children should protect the account password and must not forward one-time links to unrelated people.

## rights — Rights of the child and guardian

To the extent provided by applicable law, a child or guardian may:

- learn the categories, volume and principal uses of the child's personal information;
- request access, a copy, correction or supplementation;
- request deletion when information is no longer necessary, unlawfully processed or otherwise required to be deleted;
- close the child's account;
- restrict or object to particular processing;
- obtain an explanation of CUAC's processing rules; and
- request internal review or complain about an outcome.

CUAC will not impose unreasonable conditions on a reasonable request. To avoid disclosing a child's information to an unauthorised person, CUAC uses identity and guardianship checks proportionate to the risk. If CUAC refuses a request, it will explain the reason and provide a review route.

## withdrawal — Withdrawing guardian consent

A guardian may withdraw consent through a verified account route or `privacy@cuca.com`. CUAC's proposed internal targets are to acknowledge within one business day, complete initial verification within three business days, and ordinarily complete the request within 15 calendar days. A complex case should not exceed 30 calendar days and the reason should be explained in advance. These are proposed CUAC service targets and require confirmation by the actual operating entity and legal reviewer before publication.

Once withdrawal is verified, CUAC stops future processing that depends on that consent, restricts the child account from creating new data, and deletes or anonymises information that is no longer necessary and has no other lawful retention basis. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

If minimum records must remain for cybersecurity, audit, dispute resolution or legal obligations, CUAC restricts their purpose and access and explains the category, reason and duration. Where information has been provided to a processor or university, CUAC acts within the applicable responsibility boundary to notify the recipient. A university acting as an independent processor may require the guardian to submit a request directly to that university.

## changes — Changes to these Rules

CUAC records the version, effective date and next review date of these Rules. Material changes—especially to purposes, methods, information categories, recipients, retention or cross-border arrangements—are reassessed for their effect on children and receive renewed guardian consent where legally required.

A withdrawn, expired or unapproved version is not displayed publicly and cannot support a new guardian decision. The Simplified Chinese and English versions are reviewed and published independently. If they conflict, CUAC pauses processing that depends on the disputed wording and corrects and re-reviews the text rather than silently assigning priority to one language.
